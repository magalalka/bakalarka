FAQ

Before you call, a few answers

How long does a full AC installation take? +

Most single-family homes are done in one day, start to finish, including removal of the old unit and a full system test before we leave.

Do I need a permit to replace my system? +

In most Central Texas municipalities, yes — and we handle the permit filing as part of every installation, no extra paperwork on your end.

What SEER rating should I get? +

We size and recommend based on your home's square footage, insulation, and your budget — not just the highest number on the shelf.

Is financing really 0%? +

For qualified homeowners on full system replacements, yes. We'll walk you through eligibility during your free quote.

Do you charge more for nights and weekends? +

No. A broken AC doesn't wait for business hours, so neither does our pricing.
